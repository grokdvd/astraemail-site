Features

Everything for outreach that works

Built for freelancers, agencies and small teams who send email from their own brand.

Your own domain or Gmail

Connect Gmail, or any business mailbox over SMTP, and send from contact@yourcompany.com.

Open tracking

See when a message is opened, with filtering of automatic prefetches so numbers stay honest.

Link click tracking

Every link is tracked and checked for safety, so you know who is actually interested.

Instant phone alerts

Get a push notification the moment someone opens or clicks.

Campaigns and contacts

Manage contacts, reuse templates with variables like first name and company, and send to a whole list.

Scheduled sending

Pick the time. Messages go out from the server even if your phone is off.

One-click unsubscribe

Campaign emails carry an unsubscribe link, so you respect people's choices and keep your reputation clean.

Private by design

Mailbox credentials and tokens are stored encrypted on the server and are never sent back to the app.

Arabic and English

How it works

From install to first open in minutes

No servers to set up and no code to write.

Connect your mailbox

Sign in, then add Gmail or your domain mailbox. The app detects the provider for you.

Write and send

Compose a message or pick a template, choose your contacts, send now or schedule.

Watch the results

Follow opens and clicks per message and per recipient, and get alerts on your phone.

Honest tracking

Numbers you can trust, with the limits stated

Email tracking is never perfect. AstraEmail tells you what it can and cannot know.

  • Shown as "Open Detected", because some mail apps load images automatically.
  • Automatic prefetches and scanners are flagged so they do not inflate your rate.
  • Clicks are counted on every tracked link, with a safe redirect.
